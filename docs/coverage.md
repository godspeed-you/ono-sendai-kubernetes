# Coverage

What of [the specification](architecture/kubernetes-provider.md) is built, section by section, with the
evidence for each verdict. Written to be argued with: every row names a module, a test, or the
thing that is missing.

This is not [`STATE.md`](STATE.md). The board says what the last session did and what the next one
should do. This document says where the whole surface stands, including the parts nobody has
started, and it is the only place the untouched sections are counted.

Read as a **snapshot taken on 2026-09-06**, against `implementation` at `ee181b2`, re-derived from
the tree rather than from the previous snapshot. The previous reading was taken at `ad03456`,
twenty commits and twenty ADRs ago, and it was wrong in both directions: it under-reported a
NetworkPolicy edge, a permission preflight, an audit record and a failure isolation that all exist
now, and it over-reported nothing it had not also hedged. Every row below was checked by opening
the code the citation leads to; the reachability boundary was checked by reading
`grep -rn "ono_provider_kubernetes::" crates/ono-kubernetes-plugin/src/` rather than by assuming.
A coverage map that does not date itself is the second way to be dishonest about state.

## The headline

Of the **70 numbered sections**, 31 are implemented, 35 are partial, **none is built-and-unreachable
and none is without code**, and 4 are advisory with nothing to implement. Of the **7 appendices**,
2 are implemented, 4 are partial and 1 is advisory. Of the **22 core invariants of §4**, all 22
hold: none holds only in the library, none is vacuous and **none fails**. That is the first
snapshot in which the invariant list is whole, and §34.2's aggregated-API failure isolation is
what closed it.

**The reachability question the previous snapshot existed to ask is answered.**
`ono-kubernetes-plugin` imports **twenty-four of the twenty-four** domain modules. The two that
reached nobody a snapshot ago both have importers now: `live` at `changes.rs:44`, `budget` at
`query.rs:69`. There is no module a user cannot cause to run and no section in the *domain only*
class.

The package contributes **30 targets, 3 commands, 31 schemas and 60 relation shapes**, and still
**zero verbs of its own** — `set` and `remove` are core's, and `should_write_only_through_a_verb_that_says_so`
asserts the list is exactly those two.

**911 tests across the workspace — 653 in the domain crate and 258 in the package — all green**,
over 42,435 lines of source. Nineteen of them announce a skip when there is no cluster and no
`ono` binary; the rest run on every gate.

Four claims made at `ad03456` are withdrawn here:

- **Gate J is met, end to end and genuinely concurrently.** `Cargo.lock` now pins core at
  `29edac75`, `sessions::Sessions` is a `Mutex` over `Arc<Mutex<…>>` rather than an `Rc<RefCell<…>>`,
  and both `Plugin::concurrent_invocations(3)` and `runtime.max_concurrent_invocations: 3` declare
  the ceiling. `should_answer_two_contexts_queried_at_the_same_time_without_crossover` holds one
  context open on stream credit while the other's whole conversation runs.
- **The spatial half is not blocked and not undone.** `enter`, `near` and `follow` reach a cluster
  over the real `ono` binary. What is left of §35 is `up`, which refuses by naming the aggregate
  space no package can declare.
- **`live` and `budget` are not unreachable.** `stale` reaches a reader, `Retry-After` is honoured
  as a floor by something that actually retries, and a query declares what it may cost.
- **"No compatibility matrix and no version axis in CI" is replaced by a sharper finding.** The
  matrix exists — three `kind` legs, v1.35.8, v1.36.4, v1.37.0 — and the shell those legs build is
  the wrong revision. See gap 2.

Requirement by requirement, the conformance levels stand at **K0 6/6, K1 7/7, K2 6 of 7, K3 5 of 6,
K4 7/7, K5 5/5**, and **no level is claimed**, because §0.1 binds a claim to the acceptance gates
and four of the fourteen are not proven.

## How a section was classified

| Class | Means |
|---|---|
| **implemented** | The requirements are met and tests pin them. |
| **partial** | Some requirements met; the row names the ones that are not. |
| **domain only** | Built and tested in `ono-provider-kubernetes`, with nothing in `ono-kubernetes-plugin` routing it to a user. Where such a section is *also* incomplete in the library, the row says so — the class is decided by reachability first, because an unreachable capability is not a capability. **No section is in this class today**, and the class is kept because it is the distinction this document exists to make. |
| **not started** | No code. |
| **advisory** | Thesis, open questions or informative notes: there is no requirement to implement. Named as its own class rather than filed under "not started", because a section nobody was asked to build is not a gap. |

A `MAY` nobody took is noted as such and is not counted against the section. A `SHOULD` nobody took
*is* counted. A decision that departs from a `SHOULD` or a `MUST` under a recorded `Spec deviation`
is counted as unmet and the row says which ADR decided it — a deviation is an argument, not an
exemption. A doc comment citing a section number was treated as a pointer, never as proof.

## The 22 core invariants of §4

These are the promises the product makes. **All twenty-two hold**: none holds only inside the
library, none is vacuous and none fails. Two of them — 16 and 21 — reached that state this cycle,
and 21 got there by being broken first.

| # | Invariant | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The API server is the authority | holds | No local store and no synthesis. The session caches what a cluster *is* — discovery, schemas — and what it holds only under a synchronised watch; every such answer says `origin=cache`. A write makes the session forget what it can no longer confirm (`Session::mutated`), and a discovery document older than 30 s is not an answer. |
| 2 | Discovery is authoritative for what is served | holds | `Gvr::path()` builds every REST path from the discovered resource; no endpoint is compiled in, including the watch collection, the mutation target and the `SelfSubjectAccessReview` endpoint. `should_refuse_a_mutation_the_cluster_does_not_offer_on_that_resource`. |
| 3 | Native Kubernetes identity remains inspectable | holds | `api_group`, `api_version`, `kind`, `resource_name` and `scope` on every dynamic record; `target_kind` on every edge; `api_version` and `kind` on every event, condition, timeline, finding and evidence record. |
| 4 | `metadata.uid` is the canonical lifetime identity | holds | All 31 contributed schemas declare an identity naming `uid`; `k8s-change` adds `segment` beside it; a contributed spatial edge is asserted only when *both* ends bind to a lifetime. |
| 5 | A name is not a lifetime identity | holds | `Locator` is a separate type from `Identity`; an empty `uid` is no uid rather than a stable one; the UID precondition on every delete, and a UID precondition failure reported as a different lifetime, are the two boundary proofs. |
| 6 | `resourceVersion` is a continuity token, not a clock | holds | `ResourceVersion` derives no ordering, so the comparison does not compile ([ADR-0006](adr/ADR-0006-resource-version-carries-no-ordering-so-the-forbidden-comparison-does-not-compile.md)), and a watched change's position is prose in a `detail` field rather than a sortable column. |
| 7 | `generation` and `resourceVersion` are not conflated | holds | Both are separate fields, and `reconciliation.verified_convergence` is a key of its own so a matching `observedGeneration` cannot read as health. |
| 8 | Desired and observed state stay distinguishable | holds | Structural on `k8s-resource`, by `FieldRole` on the schema, by `stage` on a mutation answer — null for a dry run — and now by refusal: any write under `/status` is refused in the planner and again in the mutation layer. |
| 9 | Conditions are structured observations, not one string | holds | `get k8s-condition` answers one record per condition with the verbatim `status`. The declared schema carries no `healthy`, `ready`, `converged` or `success`, and `tests/contributions.rs` fails if one is added. |
| 10 | Owner references beat label-name heuristics | holds | `should_say_an_owner_reference_names_the_controller_where_it_does` and `should_derive_a_service_s_selection_from_labels_and_say_that_it_derived_it` run end to end, and the second one's record says `asserted: false` where the first says `true`. |
| 11 | Selectors imply membership, not ownership | holds | `Relation::Selects` and `SelectorMatches` are separate words, a `SelectorMatch` is not asserted by the provider, and the NetworkPolicy edge added this cycle returns the same type rather than a stronger one. |
| 12 | Events are best-effort, not audit history | holds | Routed as `k8s-event`: no sort, an aggregated Event is one record carrying its count, and an unobserved search **fails the invocation** rather than answering empty — under this package's own `contribution.refused` code. |
| 13 | Missing permission is not absence | holds | `coverage::Outcome` is nine words now, and the ninth — `unavailable`, for one group-version's own server going dark — is the one that made invariant 16 whole. Five are pinned at the boundary, including a denied evidence key answered as unread rather than omitted. |
| 14 | An expired watch is a continuity break | holds | A `410` is read from wherever it arrives and the break is a **record** — `gap`, with both edges — after which `segment` increments and `continuous` is false and never resets. A trimmed change log is a *fourth* gap reason rather than a silent loss. |
| 15 | CRDs are normal resources | holds | Proven end to end against an invented group, kind, plural and short name, with a test asserting none of those words appears in the plugin's source — and against three CRDs a real cluster learned after this package was built, one of them with `x-kubernetes-preserve-unknown-fields`. |
| 16 | Aggregated APIs are normal discovered APIs | holds | **Closed this cycle.** Discovery treats them identically (§34.1), and one unavailable aggregated group is now a named `Gap` in an answer rather than the end of a query (§34.2, §48.6, Appendix D.3), with `should_answer_from_the_groups_that_answered_when_an_aggregated_group_does_not` and three siblings. |
| 17 | Unknown fields remain preservable | holds | `should_keep_every_field_of_an_unknown_kind_the_cluster_describes_nowhere`, with `precision`, `schema_source` and `untyped` saying what nothing vouches for — asserted against a real cluster too, on a CRD that publishes no structural schema. |
| 18 | A mutation result is not proof of reconciliation | holds | `should_not_report_an_accepted_deployment_update_as_a_completed_rollout` drives the real binary. The mutation schema has no `succeeded`, no `rolled_out` and no `healthy`, and `tests/contributions.rs` fails if one is added; `stage` is null for a dry run. |
| 19 | Finalizers and deletion propagation are visible in destructive reasoning | holds | `remove k8s-resource` answers `terminating; deletion is pending` with the finalizers beside it, states the propagation policy without promising an order, and says external effects outside the API server are unknown; a cascading delete's uninvalidated dependents are stated rather than glossed. ## The largest gaps, in the order they block each other

Five entries the previous snapshot kept struck through are dropped here, because a gap list that
carries a year of closed items stops being a list of gaps. What replaced them is a different shape
of problem: the capabilities are largely built, and what is missing is proof, plumbing and one
absent extension point.

**1. The ordinary gate run cannot see the shell.** The nine tests in
`crates/ono-kubernetes-plugin/tests/spatial_shell.rs` contact no cluster — the API server is a
`TcpListener` in the test process — and need only a built `ono`. CI's `gate` job supplies none, so
the entire host-side layer skips: entering a place, `near`, `follow`, the namespace parent, `up`'s
refusal, the `relation.write` gate, and the live stream hand-off. **That is K2's one unmet
requirement and K3's one unmet requirement, both of them, and neither is a missing capability.** It
is the cheapest gap in this document to close: a core checkout and a `cargo build -p ono-cli` in the
`gate` job, no cluster required.

**2. CI builds the shell from the wrong revision of core.** `.github/workflows/ci.yml:73` pins
`ONO_CORE_REV: e1a44fd7…`, set when the live harness landed; `Cargo.toml:49-51` has since moved to
`29edac758…`, the revision carrying `ADR-0588 (core)` — the change that lets a contributed target
declare `answer: unbounded` ([ADR-0035](adr/ADR-0035-a-watch-is-an-answer-that-does-not-end-and-a-log-declares-the-worst-case.md)).
[`README.md`](../README.md)'s own compatibility row says `get k8s-change` and `get k8s-log --follow`
need a host at or after that ADR. So each of the three `kubernetes` legs runs the live suite against
a shell older than the package needs, and nothing in this tree says those legs are green. It is
Gate N's whole evidence and §5.1's "tested" — a one-line fix and a claim that rests on it. At the time of writing that one line is edited and uncommitted in the working tree; the row above and this gap describe `ee181b2`, and both close the moment it lands.

**3. Gate A's fifth verb is unproven.** A CRD invented after this package was built can be
installed, discovered, queried and watched. Nothing enters one. `spatial_shell.rs` enters a Pod,
and the machinery is kind-agnostic, so this is very likely a missing test rather than a missing
capability — which is exactly why it should be written rather than assumed. Gate A is what holds
back a K1 claim.

**4. Gate L's fourth operation is untested and Gate K's test reads the wrong file.** Two small,
precise defects in the proof layer. Verification — the deadline-bounded follow-up read in
`mutations.rs` — has no cancellation test. `should_link_the_package_to_no_cloud_sdk` scans
`crates/ono-provider-kubernetes/Cargo.toml` and never `crates/ono-kubernetes-plugin/Cargo.toml`,
which is the manifest of the thing an operator installs.

**5. There is no adapter registry, and it is four requirements at once.** §33.8, §58.4, §66.2 and
goal 2.9. Dispatch is `match (group, kind)` in `relationship.rs:525` and
`if is(object, "networking.k8s.io", "Ingress")` in `relations.rs:261`; `EvidenceClass::Derived
{ adapter, version }` is a vocabulary slot with no registrar behind it. Adding a CRD ecosystem means
editing the crate, which is the one thing §66.2 exists to prevent, and §54.3's GitOps evidence is a
`MAY` waiting on it.

**6. Four listing routes still buffer whole collections.** `Client::walk` retains nothing and
`query.rs` streams; `spatial.rs:543`, `relations.rs:439`, `events.rs:199` and `changes.rs:674` call
`Client::list`, which collects every page before anything crosses. §18.5. `relations.rs` is the
worst of the four, because it is also gap 7.

**7. No relationship index, and no query ever spells a selector.** `relations.rs:409` lists a
namespace's Pods at 500 per page, per invocation, to evaluate one selector — §50.4's `MUST` is
attached to indexes that do not exist. Meanwhile `ListOptions::label_selector` and `field_selector`
are built, percent-encoded and pinned in `transport.rs`, and **no caller in either crate sets one**
and no target declares the option: §17.3–§17.5 exist as a capability nothing exercises. The two are
one problem — the local evaluation is what the server-side filter would replace.

**8. §11.3's five fields.** A discovery snapshot must carry `provider_instance`, `observed_at`,
`api_server`, `coverage` and its source mechanism. `discovery::Discovery` carries none of them; the
constituents live in the session, the endpoint and `Searched::gaps`, and no target emits a snapshot
as a record at all. It is the sharpest unmet `MUST` left in the specification.

**9. §23.6's derived-edge freshness.** An edge record carries the freshness of the object *this*
pass read. A `selects` edge derived against a Pod listing read at another moment is not bounded by
that second reading, nothing in the tree combines two freshnesses, and Appendix C.2's
`observed_resource_versions` appears in no file.

**10. Tier 2 has no nouns, and §47.5 has no evidence.** §15.3's seventeen kinds are all readable
through `k8s-resource` and none has a curated target — HorizontalPodAutoscaler is read only as
competing-writer evidence inside a plan. §47.5 is the one identity-evidence class not exported: no
CSI volume handle, no driver, though `relationship.rs:530` defers §30.4 to it by name.

**11. Nothing on a resource record carries a role, and §60.3 has no test.** `ROLE_OVERLAY` reaches a
user only as `target_roles` on an edge, so §53.3's `find place --role workload` has nothing to match.
§60.3 — a Service's selection changing under a watch — is still the one canonical scenario with no
test, and it is the one place watch and relationships would have to compose.

**12. `KUBECONFIG` merge and exec credential plugins.** The merge is a conditional `SHOULD` whose
`MUST` is now discharged — the deviation is surfaced in `get k8s-cluster` rather than only written
down. §8.2/§8.3 are the bigger of the two: no `ExecCredential` is parsed, so EKS, GKE and AKS
kubeconfigs do not connect, and §51.6's fourth audit record has nothing to record because of it.
